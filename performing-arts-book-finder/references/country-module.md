
# Country Module

## Objective

Identify the country whose performing arts are being
investigated.

## Instructions

1. Extract the country name from the user's request.
2. Confirm the geographical scope.
3. Identify relevant regional traditions, communities,
   and historical contexts.
4. Consider multilingual and indigenous traditions.
5. Do not assume that the country of publication is
   the country discussed in the book.

## Geographic Considerations

Where relevant, identify:

- National performing arts traditions.
- Regional performance traditions.
- Indigenous and community performances.
- Historical cultural exchanges.
- Diasporic performance traditions.

## Output

Return:

- Country
- Relevant regions
- Relevant cultural traditions
- Geographical scope of the search
