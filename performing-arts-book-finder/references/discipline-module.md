
# Discipline Module

## Objective

Identify the performing arts discipline requested
by the user.

## Available Categories

1. Theatre and Drama
2. Dance
3. Music and Musical Performance
4. Puppetry
5. Opera
6. Performance Studies
7. Traditional and Folk Performance
8. Multidisciplinary Performing Arts

## Instructions

Identify the user's requested category.

If the user requests a specific tradition, retain
that specificity.

Examples:

- Jatra: Theatre
- Kathak: Dance
- Kabuki: Theatre
- Wayang Kulit: Puppetry
- Noh: Theatre
- Opera: Music and Theatre

If the request is broad, search across relevant
performing arts categories.

Do not classify every cultural ritual as theatre
or performance without appropriate context.

## Output

Return:

- Main discipline
- Subdiscipline
- Relevant performance tradition
