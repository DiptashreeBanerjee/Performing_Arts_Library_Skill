
# Academic Filter Module

## Objective

Filter books according to academic and research
preferences.

## Classification

Classify books into:

1. Academic research
2. Historical study
3. Theoretical study
4. Cultural documentation
5. Introductory or reference book

## Academic Criteria

Consider:

- Author's academic or professional credentials
- Publisher's reputation
- Scholarly references and bibliography
- Research methodology
- Critical engagement
- University or research relevance

## Switch Behaviour

If academic_only is ON:

Include books that meet credible academic
or scholarly standards.

If academic_only is OFF:

Include relevant introductory, historical,
cultural, and general-interest books as well.

Do not describe a book as academic merely because
it is published by a well-known publisher.

## Output

Provide the classification and a short justification.
