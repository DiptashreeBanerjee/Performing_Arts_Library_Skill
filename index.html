About the project

Performing Arts Book Finder is a modular LLM skill that discovers and recommends books on the performing arts of any country you choose.

Purpose of the Skill

  • Finds books by country and discipline: theatre, dance, music, puppetry, opera and performance studies.
  • Separates academic research from general reading.
  • Verifies titles, authors, publishers, years and ISBNs against reliable catalogues.
  • Returns structured recommendations with source links and access information.

Modularity

Six modules work in sequence: Country, Discipline, Book Discovery, Academic Filter, Verification and Output. Six switches shape each search: Academic Books Only, Historical Focus, Traditional Performing Arts, Contemporary Performing Arts, Open Access Preference and Strict Source Verification.

Speciality: The skill never invents bibliographic details. If a book cannot be verified, it is marked as not verified or left out.

How can you use it?

  • Go to the 'Search Your Book' Section.
  • Customise the fields accordingly.
  • Simply copy and paste it in the search box of any LLM.

Example queries

  • Find books on theatre in India.
  • Find academic books on dance in Japan.
  • Find books on puppetry in Indonesia.
  • Find books on contemporary music in Nigeria.
