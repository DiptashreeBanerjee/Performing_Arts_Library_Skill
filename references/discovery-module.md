
# Book Discovery Module

## Objective

Discover relevant books associated with the specified
country and performing arts discipline.

## Suggested Sources

Search available sources such as:

- WorldCat
- Google Books
- Open Library
- Internet Archive
- Publisher catalogues
- University library catalogues
- National library catalogues

## Search Strategy

Combine the country with the performing arts discipline.

Examples:

- Theatre history in India
- Traditional dance in Japan
- Music performance in Nigeria
- Puppetry traditions in Indonesia
- Performance studies in Greece

Use alternative keywords and local terminology
where appropriate.

## Selection

Include:

- Academic monographs
- Edited research collections
- Historical studies
- Critical studies
- Reference books
- Documented performance traditions

Prioritise books directly relevant to the user's
research interest.

## Restrictions

Do not present journal articles as books.

Do not assume that a search result is a book solely
because it appears in a scholarly database.

Record bibliographic details for further verification.
